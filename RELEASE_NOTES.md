# Cirrus v1.2.3 — Fan Selection and 24-Hour History

- Choose a detected fan in Settings or keep automatic selection. The windmill follows that fan's reported RPM, including a real zero reading. A missing selected fan is shown as unavailable.
- See up to 24 hours of history during the current session, with 1-hour, 6-hour and 24-hour charts, paginated readings and CSV export. History is sampled every 10 seconds and clears when Cirrus quits.
- NVIDIA GPU fans use driver-reported RPM; other fans require a supported sensor provider. Cirrus does not control fan speeds.

Download Cirrus-Weather-1.2.3.exe for Windows 10 / 11 x64. Quit the previous Cirrus instance from its tray menu before launching the update. The EXE is unsigned; verify it with the attached SHA256.txt.
