# Cirrus v1.2.6 — Rainbow After Rain

- Increase the Window brightness slider maximum to 120%, while preserving the existing 60% minimum and saved settings.
- After more than an hour of continuous rain or thunderstorm, a rainbow appears when the live weather turns sunny. A cloudy transition can last up to ten minutes; the rainbow lasts up to five minutes while the sky stays sunny.
- Rainbow timing resets when observations are interrupted or the selected weather source changes. The rainbow is purely visual and does not alter sensor readings or weather history.

Download Cirrus-Weather-1.2.6.exe for Windows 10 / 11 x64. Quit the previous Cirrus instance from its tray menu before launching the update. The EXE is unsigned; verify it with the attached SHA256.txt.
